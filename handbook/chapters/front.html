Foreword

Time alters all. It permeates through everything without reverence, ultimately destroying even the most fundamental bindings created by fate and destiny itself. The logical becomes irrational, the familiar uncanny, the matter-of-fact, wrong.

You may have felt this, as you grew to know your parents as flawed men, as you watched them slowly decay and die; as your birthplace was changed- ravaged by war or the stupidity of its residents.

You may have even seen it as the world changed, time and time again; and yet eerily, remains the same. As if some unseen hand, desperately trying to gather all the infinitely miniscule specs of sand, to pour them back into a shattered hourglass.

But even that is not enough. Nothing is.

Time alters all- There is no end, only change. Relentless, merciless change towards the unknown, the uncanny, the preposterous and the obscene.

Vie against it as you like, support it even, ye disillusioned fools. Eventually you will see time for what it really is.

Immortality. A fate far worse than Death.
